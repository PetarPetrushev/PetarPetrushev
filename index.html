Engineering Background

Full stack engineering with systems discipline.

I build and maintain production software across the full spectrum: managing server hardware, designing mail transfer protocols, creating RESTful backends, and structuring clean frontend interfaces.

My approach focuses on ground-up ownership. Rather than assembling fragile third-party SaaS wrappers, I engineer reliable systems from first principles. That means understanding how the underlying Linux operating system handles sockets, how mail servers route traffic reliably, and how modern web clients render interfaces efficiently.

Whether deploying high-availability email clusters with Postfix and Dovecot, integrating multi-model APIs via OpenRouter, or architecting client websites with PHP, Python, and modern JavaScript, performance and reliability are the primary targets.

I am also an active tech tinkerer who genuinely enjoys exploring new tools and architectures. Beyond core client work, you will frequently find me spinning up virtual private servers, testing different cloud providers, deploying edge functions with Cloudflare Workers, running self-hosted services, and engineering custom bots and integrations with third-party APIs like Stripe payment workflows, OpenRouter, and Discord bot APIs.

Importantly, I have been coding for years before the AI boom. My technical foundations were established by building, configuring, and debugging software manually without AI assistance. I understand programming languages, networking protocols, and systems architectures from the ground up, which means I know exactly how things work under the hood with or without an assistant.

I take pride in clean code, strict email deliverability (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), robust failover configurations, and long-term maintainability.
